Results: three variants, same faults

B1 naive fixer: same detection, then just fixes. B0 strong rules: everything ReVive has except the uncertainty gate, so it acts on its best guess. B2 ReVive: full system. Dev = 10 scenarios x 30 seeds. Held-out = 7 frozen scenarios x 30 seeds, never tuned against.

Safety vs autonomy

On clear cases B0 matches ReVive. The difference is the ambiguous case: B0 guesses and acts, ReVive escalates.

Is the uncertainty gate honest?

Synthetic incidents with a hidden true cause. Threshold set on 100 dev incidents, checked on 200 held-out. The gate's set contains the true cause 95% of the time (target 90%) and asks a human on about 15% of incidents.
